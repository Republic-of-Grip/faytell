What this is

Faytell

A window for what a comment field can't hold. When a conversation somewhere else needs a picture, a film, an infographic or a longer line of reasoning, I put it here and leave the link there.

One page per thought

Each page holds one idea, shown the way it needs to be shown: text, images, diagrams or video. It carries a date and a link back to the conversation it belongs to, so it stays tied to its moment.

Chosen, not generated

Nothing here writes itself. Every page is put up by hand, on purpose. If you followed a link to get here, it's because I chose to share it.

Out in the open

The pages and their history live in a public repository. Anyone can see how a page was made and what changed.
